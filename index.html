About Me

Design is a dialogue with space.

I'm CALEB GOMEZ, Im a software architect and spatial designer based in Mariveles Bataan Philippines. For over a decade, I've been shaping the built environment — from intimate residential retreats to civic landmarks — with a philosophy rooted in material honesty and contextual sensitivity.

My practice bridges the gap between technical rigor and poetic intention. Every project begins with listening: to the land, the client, the community.
